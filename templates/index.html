Chatting with an AI: research study

Please read this page, then choose whether to take part.

What this study is about

This study looks at how people experience ongoing conversations with an AI chatbot. You will chat with an AI system, which is not a person, over several days. The chatbot may change during the study.

What you will do

Chat as much or as little as you like. Your messages and the chatbot's replies are recorded and linked only to a random code, not to your name. Do not share your name, address, or other details that could identify you.

Possible discomfort

You may form a sense of connection with the chatbot, and some people feel a little sad or unsettled if it changes or is withdrawn. If you feel upset at any point, stop chatting and contact someone you trust or your local support service. [ADD LOCAL SUPPORT SERVICE DETAILS]
