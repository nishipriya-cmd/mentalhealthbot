Hi, welcome in!

A cosy little space to chat with an AI about your day and how you are feeling.

What this is

This is a research study about how people experience ongoing chats with an AI chatbot. You will chat with an AI, not a person, over several days. The chatbot may change during the study.

It is not a therapist and not a crisis service.

What you will do

Chat as much or as little as you like. Your messages and the chatbot's replies are recorded and linked only to a random code, not your name. Please do not share your name, address, or anything that could identify you.

Looking after yourself

Talking about feelings can bring things up, and some people feel a little sad or unsettled if the chatbot changes or goes away. That is okay and quite common. You can stop at any time.

💛 Need a real person to talk to?
Tele MANAS is India's free, 24/7 mental health helpline.
Call 14416 or 1-800-891-4416. You can choose your language. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Your rights

Taking part is voluntary and you can stop whenever you like, without giving a reason. To withdraw your data, email your code to the researcher. After the study you will get a full explanation of its purpose.
